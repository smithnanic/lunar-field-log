# Lunar 100 Field Log

A one-target-at-a-time helper for working through the Astronomical League's
[Lunar Observing Program](https://www.astroleague.org/lunar-observing-program/).

Live site: https://smithnanic.github.io/lunar-field-log/

- The moon photo is shaded to match the phase on the chosen night.
- Each target is tinted on the photo, with the dates it is likely to be sunlit.
- Swipe (or use the arrow keys) to move between targets, and tap **Found it** to log one.
- The log is kept in your browser only. Use **Menu → Open my log → Copy log as CSV** to take it elsewhere.

This is an unofficial helper. The checklist belongs to the Astronomical League.
The moon photograph is a 27-panel mosaic taken on 24 November 2026 with a Celestron
NexStar Evolution 8 EdgeHD and a ZWO ASI585MC.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app (markup, styles, script and feature data).
- `moon.jpg`: the moon photograph.
